# Fotografias fictícias do dashboard

Asset final: `frontend/public/images/demonstracao-equipe.webp` (1536 × 1024, oito retratos em um atlas de quatro colunas e duas linhas). A apresentação seleciona cada retrato por CSS, sem atribuir fotografias fictícias a colaboradores reais.

Criado com a skill imagegen e a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, sem CLI. O PNG original foi preservado no diretório de imagens geradas; o WebP utilizado pela aplicação está no repositório.

Prompt final:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single photo atlas sprite sheet for a mining dashboard demo, NOT a UI mockup. Create exactly eight different fictional adult Brazilian mining workers photographed as employee badge portraits, arranged in a perfectly aligned borderless 4-column by 2-row grid. Each of the eight equal cells is a vertical head-and-shoulders 3:4 portrait, overall canvas 3:2 (1536x1024). Distinct people: six men and two women of diverse Brazilian skin tones, ages 25-55; front-facing, eyes open, neutral approachable expression, white hard hats with small headlamp, navy work shirt and orange reflective vest, two brigadists in red hard hats. Face and entire helmet centered with adequate space inside each cell. Realistic skin texture, soft industrial photographic light, subtly blurred neutral dark gray backgrounds. No dividers, borders, gutters, written names, numbers, company logos, labels, watermark, or other text. These are entirely fictional people for clearly labelled demonstration software.
```
